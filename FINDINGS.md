# Findings

Using the fixed synthetic dataset generated for this portfolio:

- Overall churn rate: **25.3%**
- Month-to-month contract churn: **39.1%**
- One-year contract churn: **9.7%**
- Two-year contract churn: **7.0%**
- Highest-churn payment method: **Electronic check (31.0%)**
- Churn is materially higher among month-to-month customers and customers without tech support.

## Interpretation
The analysis suggests contract commitment, support experience and payment method are useful segmentation dimensions for retention work. Because the data is synthetic, these are portfolio findings rather than claims about a real company.
